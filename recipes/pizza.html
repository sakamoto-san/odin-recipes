Pizza Recipe

Description

Pizza is a globally beloved culinary masterpiece originating from Naples, Italy, celebrated for its perfect harmony of simple, high-quality ingredients. At its core lies a leavened wheat dough crust, which is meticulously stretched and baked at intense temperatures to achieve a texture that is simultaneously crisp on the outside and airy on the inside. This canvas is traditionally layered with a vibrant, savory sauce made from crushed tomatoes and topped with rich, melted mozzarella cheese. Baked quickly in a wood-fired or deck oven, the ingredients fuse together into a hot, bubbling dish that delivers comfort and satisfaction in every bite.

The true magic of pizza lies in its incredible versatility and cultural adaptability across the globe. Beyond the classic Margherita, it serves as a blank canvas for infinite flavor combinations, ranging from smoky pepperoni and fresh vegetables to gourmet additions like prosciutto, arugula, and truffle oil. Different regions have birthed their own iconic styles, such as the thick, buttery crust of a Chicago deep-dish, the foldable slices of a New York pie, and the delicate, charred edges of authentic Neapolitan pizza. This endless adaptability ensures that pizza remains a timeless favorite for casual weeknight dinners, celebratory gatherings, and late-night cravings alike.

Ingredients

  • 1 ½ cups warm water (110 degrees F/45 degrees C)
  • 2 ¼ teaspoons active dry yeast
  • ½ teaspoon brown sugar
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 3 ⅓ cups all-purpose flour, divided

Steps

  1. Stir together warm water, yeast, and brown sugar in a large mixing bowl; let sit for 10 minutes.
  2. Stir oil and salt into yeast mixture. Mix in 2 1/2 cups flour until incorporated. Turn dough out onto a clean, floured surface. Knead dough, adding remaining flour, a little at a time, until dough is no longer sticky. Place dough into an oiled bowl.
  3. Cover with a towel and let rise until doubled in size, about 1 hour.
  4. Punch down dough and form it into a tight ball. Allow dough to relax for 1 minute before rolling out.
  5. Preheat the oven to 425 degrees F (220 degrees C).
  6. If baking dough on a pizza stone, place toppings on dough and bake immediately. If baking dough on a pan, lightly oil the pan and let dough rise for 15 to 20 minutes before topping and baking it.
  7. Bake in the preheated oven until cheese is melted and crust is golden brown, 15 to 20 minutes.
